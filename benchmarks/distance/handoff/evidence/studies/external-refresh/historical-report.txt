# Refreshed native/external distance-search comparison

The native implementations remain competitive with both external tools at matched
budgets. Across 42 case/seed/budget comparisons, guided returns a lower weight
than dist-m4ri in **25**, ties **17**, and loses **none**. Against QDistEvol it wins
**29**, ties **13**, and loses **none**. These are descriptive outcomes on seven
fixed cases and three seeds per budget, not a general dominance claim.

Guided is not uniformly better than incremental: it wins **11**, ties **24**, and
loses **7**. Keep both native strategies as candidates. Dist-m4ri remains a useful
external control, including a matching Tanner median at 60 seconds.

## Median delivered upper bounds

Each cell is **10 seconds → 60 seconds/code**, split equally between X and Z.
Lower is better. Budgets use independent seeds, so these are not trajectory
prefixes and medians need not improve monotonically.

| Code | Reference target | Incremental | Guided | dist-m4ri | QDistEvol |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Board700 | 28 | 73 → 73 | 75 → 73 | 79 → 77 | 84 → 82 |
| Board682 | 72 | 83 → 80 | 81 → 80 | 88 → 84 | 94 → 91 |
| Regression690 | 28 | 82 → 80 | 84 → 80 | 87 → 84 | 92 → 92 |
| Tanner432 | 32 | 32 → 30 | 29 → 29 | 36 → 29 | 35 → 31 |
| Mitten975 | 24 | 24 → 24 | 24 → 24 | 24 → 26 | 70 → 44 |
| Toric1000 | 20 | 20 → 20 | 20 → 20 | 20 → 20 | 20 → 20 |
| Bicycle960 | 60 | 60 → 60 | 60 → 60 | 60 → 60 | 60 → 60 |

Tanner's evaluation target is the stored weight-32 witness, rather than the 33
in its case name.

The strongest relative native advantage is on the hard board/regression cases.
At 60 seconds both native medians are four weight units below dist-m4ri on each
of these three cases, and nine to twelve below QDistEvol. However, all methods
remain far from the weight-28 targets on Board700 and Regression690. The best
individual native witnesses in this screen are 68 and 77 respectively; no method
hits any of the three hard-case targets at either budget.

On Tanner, dist-m4ri's 60-second values are 29, 34, 29; guided's are 29, 29, 29.
The equal median does not imply equal recovery reliability. On Mitten, the
short-budget dist-m4ri values are 24, 26, 24; the independent longer-budget seeds
produce 30, 26, 24. That small-sample reversal is not evidence that longer search
hurts. QDistEvol's longer Mitten values are 40, 62, 44: it benefits from time but
still trails both native methods' three 24s.

## Initialization and sector results

Common initialization already gives Toric1000 weight 20 and Bicycle960 X weight
60. None of the searches improves those code-level bounds. Therefore their ties
are practical outcomes of the shared wrapper, not evidence of equal independent
search effectiveness.

The bicycle Z-sector medians illustrate the hidden difference:

| Method | 10 seconds/code | 60 seconds/code |
|---|---:|---:|
| Incremental | 154 | 142 |
| Guided | 124 | 126 |
| dist-m4ri | 164 | 158 |
| QDistEvol | 174 | 164 |

All start from the same Z-sector initialization bound of 180. The stronger
existing Z reference support of weight 152 is retained in the manifest, and is
never supplied to search. The code-level target is 60. The previously identified
X/Z symmetry transfer is outside this general-search experiment; these Z-sector
search gains do not improve the code-level bound.

## Matched protocol and scope

The [frozen plan](../../strategy_prototypes/EXTERNAL_PLAN.md) defines 168
configurations: seven existing cases, four methods and three fresh seeds at each
budget. [The 10-second report](t1-10s/REPORT.md) uses seeds 1000--1002;
[the 60-second report](t1-60s/REPORT.md) uses 1010--1012. Each report includes
per-seed X/Z values, target hits, paired outcomes and resource accounting.

Runs are serial on CPU0 of the same AMD EPYC 9554P VM, with method order shuffled
within each case/seed. Numerical libraries and Numba use one thread. Preparation,
identical logical initialization, session setup and delivered witnesses count
against the budget. Reference witnesses never enter search; there is no target
stopping. Matrix loading, imports, synthetic-toy JIT warmup, and independent
validation are outside the deadline. Validation completes before the next timed
search. Small read-only analysis jobs use CPU7 during measurement. The VM's vCPU
assignment does not establish exclusive ownership of a host physical core.

The native kernels and guided policy are unchanged. QDistEvol uses the pinned
upstream algorithm with its existing population and mutation settings, allowing
10,000 generations. Its observer passes scored results back unchanged and stops
after a scored basis reaches the deadline. dist-m4ri uses its unchanged CLI,
method 1, one thread and no target stopping, with 50 ms reserved for exit-time
witness delivery. All exported ties are retained. Native batches target 2 ms.
The common starting bound is retained by the wrapper; it is not injected into
any engine's internal incumbent or fitness.

Sources are pinned to codedistance `a4afe9c09bbf5790da9ecc05b65c5b62343979ad` and
dist-m4ri `538d119f6e98b3782415eb78f7ce322a076f8101`. The latter is built with
`-O3 -march=native`, static M4RI and no OpenMP, as recorded in its archived build
provenance. The comparison concerns these implementations and settings; no
external parameter sweep was performed. Native bases, M4RI iterations and
QDistEvol candidates are not equivalent units of work, so their counts are not
reported as cross-method speedup factors. The older Apple-machine QDistEvol
pilot is not mixed into these results.

## Evidence and verification

All **7,142** distinct-per-side/run exported supports were independently checked
and saved through the research kit: 3,457 in the short sweep and 3,685 in the long
sweep. The persistence audits match each saved document to its original emitted
support. There are **zero late witness deliveries**. Final evaluations can finish
slightly after a deadline, but only timely observations receive credit. Aggregate
CPU/elapsed ratios are approximately 0.997--1.000, including M4RI child CPU time.
Preparation plus common initialization takes roughly 22--24 ms/side at the median.

- [Short persistence audit](t1-10s/audit.json) and
  [short external-protocol audit](t1-10s/external-audit.json): passed.
- [Long persistence audit](t1-60s/audit.json) and
  [long external-protocol audit](t1-60s/external-audit.json): passed.
- [Combined audit](combined-audit.json): 168 configurations, identical source and
  binary hashes across budgets, identical complete initialization for all 14
  case/sector pairs across methods, seeds and budgets.
- Eight synthetic-input adapter tests passed; Ruff and the diff whitespace check
  passed. The tests cover unchanged QDistEvol observation semantics, deadline
  handling, retention of M4RI exports and valid witnesses from all four adapters.
- [Trusted verifier integrity](validator-integrity.txt): all 27 pinned files match.
- [Hardware](hardware.json), [installed packages](environment-freeze.txt), per-budget
  source archives, executable hashes, raw logs, CLI commands/outputs and matrices
  accompany the results. `SHA256SUMS.json` hashes this result tree.

All weights are witnessed upper bounds. Three seeds support screening, not a
reliable miss-probability estimate or an exact-distance claim. The only schema
exemption is the existing size cap for the n>700 benchmark fixtures. No full
candidate gate, leaderboard changes or publication workflow was run.

Two source-archiving setup attempts stopped before corpus search because of a
Makefile path mismatch. Their partial setup artifacts are retained under
`setup-attempt-no-search/` and `setup-attempt-2-no-search/`; neither contains a
measured search configuration. The corrected driver is archived with both grids.

## Reproducing the run and reports

With the pinned dependencies and native modules built, choose new output paths:

```sh
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/study_external.py \
  --output /tmp/external-10s-replay
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/study_external.py \
  --output /tmp/external-60s-replay --seconds 60 --seed-start 1010
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/report_external.py /tmp/external-10s-replay
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/audit_external.py /tmp/external-10s-replay
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/report_external.py /tmp/external-60s-replay
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/audit_external.py /tmp/external-60s-replay
```

The driver refuses to overwrite an output directory. A witness-save failure is a
hard error, with raw observations retained for recovery. Wall-clock runs need
not reproduce identical final weights even with the same seeds.
