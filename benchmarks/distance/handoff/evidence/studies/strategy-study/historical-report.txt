# Strategy experiment: decisions and an initialization gap

The most important practical finding is a missing cheap baseline: scanning the
canonical logical representatives produced during preparation finds **weight 60
on fresh-bb-960** and **weight 20 on toric-1000**. Both witnesses are independently
validated and saved. Neither requires random search or a reference witness.
The bicycle result beats every randomized result in this experiment.

This changes the interpretation of the timed screen. Its comparisons are valid
for the implemented warm-search interfaces, but apparent gains on these two cases
do not establish end-to-end improvement over initialization that returns the best
witness already computed. The next benchmark should include that initialization
for every method and update the bicycle reference bound from 152 to at most 60.

## What was tested

Three prototypes were implemented independently in parallel, then frozen before
evaluation. Timed methods ran serially on CPU 0: one worker, two seconds per code
split equally between X and Z, five new seeds (500–504), seven cases, and six
methods. This gives **210 configurations**. Methods continued after target recovery.
Fresh six-pivot RIS, persistent incremental RIS, and the existing circulant pass
were controls. Reference supports were never passed to search.

See the [full comparison](screen-2s/REPORT.md), the
[predeclared plan](../../strategy_prototypes/PLAN.md), and
[prototype descriptions/build instructions](../../strategy_prototypes/README.md).

## Candidates worth pursuing

| Candidate | Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Guided elite-basis search | Keep as the leading general candidate | Lower median weight than incremental RIS on all seven inputs; 25 lower / 4 equal / 6 higher matched outcomes. Excluding the two initialization-easy cases leaves 17 lower / 2 equal / 6 higher. |
| RIS plus stabilizer tabu refinement | Keep as a selective refinement stage | Tanner target recovery improved from 2/5 to 4/5. Exact seed replay confirms the local moves improved every Tanner and bicycle run. It wasted substantial time on high-check-weight cases and was weaker on Mitten. |
| Connected-region column elimination | Deprioritize this prototype | Higher weights than incremental RIS in 26/35 matched outcomes. Its reliable toric recovery adds no advantage over the already-available prepared witness. This does not rule out exact cluster methods or better structural searches. |

The clearest comparisons on cases not solved by the preparation scan are:

| Case | Incremental median | Guided median | Refinement hybrid median | Target recovery: incremental / guided / refinement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Tanner 432 | 33 | 30 | 30 | 2/5 / 4/5 / 4/5 |
| Mitten 975 | 30 | 24 | 36 | 1/5 / 3/5 / 0/5 |
| Board 700 | 77 | 75 | 77 | 0/5 / 0/5 / 0/5 |
| Regression 690 | 84 | 83 | 86 | 0/5 / 0/5 / 0/5 |

The existing circulant pass found weight **48 on the regression in every seed**,
against incremental RIS's median 84. It still missed the weight-28 target, but
remains the strongest demonstrated family-specific complement on that case.
The new guided method is an elite-basis experiment, not a faithful QDistEvol port.

## What the additional checks established

The [exact Tanner seed replay](replay-tanner/REPORT.md) reproduced the same RIS
sessions used by refinement. Their best seed weights were 39, 31, 42, 33, 42;
the timed hybrid returned 30, 29, 29, 31, 33. The
[bicycle replay](replay-bb/REPORT.md) gave seed weights 158, 160, 156, 156, 156;
the hybrid returned 110, 114, 118, 114, 116. Every replayed witness matched an
existing validated, saved document. These are fixed-work attribution checks,
not additional equal-time seeding-only controls.

The [prepared-basis diagnostic](prepared-basis/REPORT.md) exposed the stronger
bicycle and toric witnesses. A further
[logical-initialization diagnostic](logical-initialization/REPORT.md) enumerated
all nonzero combinations of chosen representatives when k <= 16, and singles
and pairs otherwise. It then refined the best representative on each side.
Combinations helped some high-k cases but did not beat the search winners;
refining that single chosen representative added no improvement in this one-seed
check. This reinforces the need for diverse starting representatives/classes.
Enumeration of representative combinations does not enumerate their stabilizer
variants and is not an exact-distance algorithm.

## Next experiment

1. Return cheap prepared/combined witnesses before randomized search, and seed
   every method with the same initial bound. Refresh the frozen reference corpus.
2. Compare incremental RIS, guidance, and guidance with a small refinement budget
   on fresh seeds. Include a seeding-only ablation and charge every stage to the
   same total budget. The combined strategy has not been tested here.
3. Expand to the existing corpus and relabeled copies, so accidental alignment
   with canonical elimination is visible. Retain a budgeted circulant pass on
   applicable cases. Larger inputs remain necessary for cache-scaling claims.

Five seeds are sufficient for screening candidates, not for establishing reliable
miss probabilities. The hard weight-28 cases remain unresolved by the general
strategies. All reported weights are witnessed upper bounds, not exact distances
or full-gate-approved code submissions.

## Validation and artifacts

Thirty new/prototype Python tests passed. Guided C++ row-space/pivot invariants
also passed ASAN/UBSAN checks. The trusted verifier's 27-file integrity check,
Ruff on the new orchestration scripts, and whitespace checks passed.

The main screen retained **139,985 emitted observations**, including 395 late
observations without deadline credit, and saved **138,718 distinct-per-side/run
supports** through the research kit. The two initialization diagnostics saved
another 140 documents. Replays reuse the original validated documents.
The original event logs, matrices, executable hashes, source archives, and
[artifact audit](screen-2s/audit.json) accompany the results.

Validation and repeated full-matrix submission packaging dominated total runtime,
especially for refinement's many seed-session histories. This is benchmark
evidence overhead, not an intrinsic cost of the local-move algorithm. A standalone
tool should retain witnesses efficiently with matrix data stored once, while
keeping the trusted submission path for repository validation.

Workspace allocation and delivery batching differ between interfaces and are
documented in the full report. These measurements do not establish a production
end-to-end speedup, scaling beyond 1,000 qubits, or superiority over unmeasured
algorithms. The original core and production verifier were not modified.
