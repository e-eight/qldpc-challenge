# Bounded structural dispatcher

Implemented a native C++ detector and a Python scheduler that route suitable
matrices to the existing full single-block search, with native guided fallback.
The previous measured search engines and study artifacts remain unchanged.

**The bounded prepass works: structural gains survive fresh column permutations,
and all 29 matched outcomes with rejected partitions tie guided search.** The
current pilot policy still over-allocates time to a weak route on Board682. Keep
the dispatcher as an explicit experimental choice until that allocation improves;
it is not a universally better replacement for guided or incremental search.

## Implementation

- [C++ detector](../../../../native/structure_dispatch/detect.cpp): uint16 overlap
  counts, cheap confidence rejection, a bijective-row-maxima fast path, and
  preallocated assignment buffers with cooperative clock checks. It avoids the
  previous full orbit enumeration and does not call SciPy assignment.
- [Scheduler/API](../../dispatch_search.py): identical common logical
  initialization; min(50 ms, 2% of sector budget) for detection; a
  min(100 ms, 10% of remaining time) route pilot; continue only if the pilot
  improves initialization, targeting a 20% reserve for guided search.
- [Build/API documentation](../../../../native/structure_dispatch/README.md),
  [synthetic tests](../../test_dispatch.py),
  [persisted benchmark driver](../../study_dispatch.py), and
  [auditor/report generator](../../audit_dispatch.py).

The detector caps input dimensions at 4096 rows/columns, row weight at 64, and
score/matching work. It rejects oversized shapes before Python input conversion.
These caps apply to detection; unsupported matrices use the existing native
fallback. The largest observed modeled detector workspace is 2,163,025 bytes,
approximately 2.06 MiB. It is temporary scratch storage, not process RSS.

The maximum observed total detection time is **10.895 ms**, including conversion
and both proposals. No detector call sequence exceeds its allotted budget in
this study. Mitten's shape rejection takes about 0.01 ms per sector. Native
allocation, kernel construction and final batches remain noninterruptible;
the deadlines and guided reserve are cooperative targets, not hard real-time
guarantees. The clock, work, and shape limits are documented in the
[frozen plan](../../strategy_prototypes/DISPATCH_PLAN.md).

## Matched results

The screen measures 13 inputs × four methods × three seeds at 2 seconds/code:
seven originals and six fresh relabelings. The second grid measures seven
originals × four methods × two different seeds at 10 seconds/code. Each budget
splits equally between X and Z. Timed configurations run serially on CPU0 with
one search thread. Common preparation, initialization, detection, setup, search,
and witness delivery all count against the deadline. Reference supports and
targets never enter search. The two budgets are independent runs, not prefixes.

Selected **2-second medians** on fresh column permutations:

| Input | Guided | Existing circulant with fallback | Dispatcher |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Board700 columns shuffled | 78 | 78 | 28 |
| Regression690 columns shuffled | 84 | 84 | 48 |
| Board700 columns and rows shuffled | 78 | 78 | 78 |
| Regression690 columns and rows shuffled | 84 | 84 | 84 |

The 28 and 48 come from the routed kernel, not initialization or fallback. They
appear in all three seeds on both original and column-permuted layouts.
Independent row shuffling still defeats structure recovery, but now leads to
prompt fallback. New permutations use RNG 1460 rather than the previous study's
RNG 1230; these are fresh layouts of known codes, not held-out code families.

**10-second medians** on the original corpus:

| Code | Incremental | Guided | Circulant with fallback | Dispatcher |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Board700 | 76 | 76 | 76 | 28 |
| Board682 | 81.5 | 81.5 | 83.5 | 83.5 |
| Regression690 | 82.5 | 82 | 48 | 48 |
| Tanner432 | 31 | 30 | 30 | 30 |
| Mitten975 | 25 | 24 | 24 | 24 |
| Toric1000 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 20 |
| Bicycle960 | 60 | 60 | 60 | 60 |

Across all **53 matched case/seed outcomes**, dispatcher versus each control:

| Control | Lower weight | Equal | Higher weight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Incremental | 30 | 17 | 6 |
| Guided | 18 | 32 | 3 |
| Circulant with fallback | 14 | 38 | 1 |

Here “lower” means a lower witnessed upper bound, not a proven lower bound on
distance. All three regressions against guided occur on Board682. With rejected
partitions, every one of the 29 paired outcomes ties guided. Tanner, Mitten, toric
and bicycle960 also match guided's median at each budget. Toric 20 and bicycle 60
already come from common initialization and are not new search gains.

routed-only outputs, decisions, fallback allocation and timing. Exact aggregate

## Remaining limitation and next refinement

Board682 illustrates the policy weakness: a route can improve a mediocre
initialization yet remain worse than generic search after more time. At two
seconds its dispatcher median is 84 versus guided 84 and incremental 82. At ten
seconds it is 83.5 versus guided 81.5. The 20% reserve sometimes rescues the route's
answer slightly, but does not eliminate the regression. A stronger next policy
would compare native and routed progress through resumable pilot sessions, then
adjust allocation. That policy has not been implemented or measured here.

Recovery still depends on useful check-row order. Confidence and two equal cycles
are routing evidence, not proofs of a code automorphism. Every accepted partition
uses kernels of the actual matrices and actual logical parity checks, and every
returned support is independently validated. None of these experiments improves
the frozen hard reference witnesses: Board700 28, Board682 72, Regression690 28.
The useful gain is automated access to known structural shortcuts with little
fallback overhead.

## Validation and reproduction

Both grids passed complete-grid, identical-initialization, source/binary/archive,
matrix/transform, route-partition, decision/export-counter, and saved-witness
identity audits. Sources and binaries are identical across the two budgets;
common initialization is also identical. All **13,519 saved witness documents**
were checked through the existing kit path. All 20 late exports are preserved
without deadline credit. Only the existing n>700 fixture size-cap exemption is
used. No trusted verifier, leaderboard, CI, commit or publication changes.

There are **29 passing unit/regression tests**: 15 new detector/scheduler/audit
tests and 14 existing wrapper/routing tests. They include independent assignment
comparison, column relabeling, deletion noise, rejection/deadline handling,
inverse support mapping, successful and rejected pilots, guided reservation,
late output, save failure propagation, and corrupted-audit rejection. See
[tests.txt](tests.txt) and [lint.txt](lint.txt).

The main grids allocate 872 seconds of search time; observed timed wall time is
876.48 seconds and CPU time 871.40 seconds, excluding imports, matrix loading and
independent validation/persistence. [hardware.json](hardware.json),
[compiler.txt](compiler.txt) and [environment-freeze.txt](environment-freeze.txt)
record the environment. The matrix-only [detection diagnostic](detection.json)
predates witness search; policy was not tuned on timed corpus results.

Build the native detector as documented, then reproduce with fresh output paths:

```sh
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/study_dispatch.py --output <new-short-output>
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/audit_dispatch.py <completed-short-output>
```

The frozen plan specifies the budgets and seeds; [the long-grid environment](original-10s/environment.json) records the exact seven original case IDs and arguments.
Each grid has raw timestamped supports, saved-candidate counts, 116 measured source
files across three source archives, binary hashes, and a runner log. A supplemental
[test dependency archive](test-dependencies.tar.gz) includes the existing shared
toy fixture and adapter; their bytes were checked against the previous frozen
routing archive. Audits require the live measured sources/binaries and local kit
staging documents. All raw supports are also retained in the grid for replay.

[validation-summary.json](validation-summary.json) indexes the checks and
[SHA256SUMS.json](SHA256SUMS.json) pins the final artifact files. These are
experimental witnessed upper bounds, not exact-distance certificates or full
candidate-gate passes.
