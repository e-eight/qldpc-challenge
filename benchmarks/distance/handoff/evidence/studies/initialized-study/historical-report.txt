# Initialization, refinement, compute budget and structural proposals

The largest improvement was construction-specific: a matrix-only cyclic-shift
pass recovered **weight 28 on board700 in 44.6 ms**, including preparation and
witness delivery. Guided search still returned **70--71 after 60 seconds**.
All of the structural pass's returned witnesses were independently validated
and saved. This is one timing observation and a witnessed upper bound, not an
exact-distance proof or a generally applicable speedup.

Adding selective local refinement to initialized guided search produced **no
better final code-level weight in 27 matched runs**: all 21 screen comparisons
and all six longer-budget comparisons tied. Keep guided as a general candidate;
this particular hybrid does not warrant becoming the default.

## What changed and what was measured

`../../initialized_search.py` composes the existing native engines with common
initialization and a small refinement pool. It scans logical representatives,
then all representative combinations for k <= 16 or all pairs for larger k.
The pool retains eight low-weight logical classes. The hybrid refines an untried
support in a 5 ms slice when it has earned enough of its 10% refinement budget.
Native trial loops and the guided population policy are unchanged.

Preparation, initialization, session creation, search and witness delivery are
inside the budget. Matrix loading, Python imports and independent verification
are outside it. Each code receives an equal X/Z split; reference supports and
target weights never enter the search. Every exported support, including
non-global and late improvements, is retained and saved through the kit.

- [10-second screen](screen-10s/REPORT.md): seven cases, three methods, three
  fresh seeds (800--802), 63 configurations.
- [60-second diagnostic](hard-60s/REPORT.md): three hard cases, guided and hybrid,
  two fresh seeds (810--811), 12 configurations. Prefixes record progress without
  restarting the run or changing its settings.
- [Cyclic-shift diagnostic](orbit-completion/REPORT.md): three hard cases in their
  original layouts and under a fixed random qubit relabeling.
- [Polynomial-division diagnostic](polynomial-completion/REPORT.md): the same six
  inputs, with a finite set of common-factor proposals.

One worker, CPU0, serial timed runs and shuffled method order per case/seed.
Validation and saving finished before the next timed search. Small development
and read-only analysis jobs were pinned to CPU7 during the timed studies; no
competing timed search ran. The native policies and main schedule were frozen
in `../../strategy_prototypes/INITIALIZED_PLAN.md` before evaluation. The two
structural diagnostics were exploratory additions, not part of the frozen grid.

## Does more compute close the gap?

These are medians from the **same two guided trajectories**, each observed at
10 and 60 seconds/code. Lower is better; fractional medians are not witnesses.

| Case | Reference target | At 10 seconds | At 60 seconds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Board700 | 28 | 74.5 | 70.5 |
| Board682 | 72 | 83.5 | 79.5 |
| Regression690 | 28 | 81.5 | 80.5 |

More time bought modest progress, not recovery of any of these targets. Two
seeds and a 60-second ceiling cannot establish asymptotic plateaus or reliable
miss probabilities. However, these measurements give no reason to expect a
small further throughput improvement to close the largest gaps by itself.

In the broader 10-second screen, guided's medians were 73, 82, 83, 29, 24, 20,
and 60 for board700, board682, regression690, Tanner432, Mitten975, toric1000
and bicycle960 respectively. It beat incremental in seven matched runs, tied
ten and lost four. Tanner was the clearest gain: median 29 versus 32.

## What structure helped?

The cyclic pass proposes simultaneous shifts of supplied check rows inside two
equal coordinate blocks. It checks every proposed row for zero syndrome and
nontrivial logical parity on the actual matrices. It does not assume that the
shift preserves the code, and receives no parent matrix or reference witness.

On board700, the supplied rows cover only part of these cyclic orbits. Completing
them exposes 116 X and 106 Z nontrivial rows, all of weight 28. The submitted
construction is a check-deletion chain, consistent with these observations.
The pass found no logical on the randomly relabeled control or on the two full
circulant examples. Recovering hidden block coordinates is **not implemented**.

The polynomial pass rotates the first check row and divides its two polynomials
by gcd(a,b) or gcd(a,b,x^L+1), testing each proposal independently. It found only
weights 234, 191 and 171 on the three original inputs and nothing on their
relabeled controls. This bare proposal generator is not useful here. Searching
linear combinations of reduced generators remains untested; the underlying
reduction is described by [Wang and Pryadko](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.17216#S3.SS1).

The existing single-block circulant search remains complementary: the preceding
matched two-second study reached 48 on regression690 in every seed. It was not
rerun or combined with the new strategies in this experiment.

## Why the local improvements do not justify the hybrid

There were no local improvements at all on the three hard cases in either new
grid. Refinement helped intermediate Tanner bounds, but guided ultimately caught
up. The cap avoided the large time waste in the earlier heavy-refinement study.

On bicycle960, refinement improves the measured Z-side median from 136 to 122,
but X initialization already gives 60, leaving the code-level bound unchanged.
Moreover, an exact matrix check shows that reversing all qubit columns maps its
X check-row set to its Z check-row set. Transferring the prepared X witness by
this symmetry already dominates the Z search. See [sector results](screen-10s/SIDES.md)
and [matrix symmetry checks](symmetry-check.json). No transformed witness was
generated in this diagnostic; the timed implementation does not use the symmetry.
The same exact row-set equality holds for board682, regression690 and toric1000.

This hybrid uses initialization and global native improvements as its seed pool.
It does not inspect every elite basis, inject refined witnesses into the native
population, or use the previous experiment's frequent independent RIS sessions.
Its negative result does not rule out all combinations of evolution and local
search. Some preparation is duplicated and the policy wrapper uses Python;
this is an experimental composition, not a fused C++ implementation.

## Recommended next work

1. Make prepared witnesses, verified sector symmetries and applicable structural
   passes part of one initialization path. Preserve a plain guided fallback.
2. For the remaining hard full-bicycle cases, test search in reduced algebraic
   subspaces with both blocks, rather than just scoring their generators.
3. Compare an independent syndrome-decoder search or higher-order information-set
   search using bounded matching lists. Those change candidate generation rather
   than only the speed of the current basis walk. See
   [theory and implementation directions](../../strategy_prototypes/NEXT_STRATEGIES.md).
4. Evaluate any resulting portfolio under one total budget on more existing
   inputs and relabelings before drawing broad conclusions. Larger n is still
   required to assess L3/main-memory scaling.

## Validation and reproduction

35 relevant tests passed, including logical classes, syndrome preservation,
word boundaries, refinement budgeting, deadline handling and evidence retention.
Ruff and whitespace checks passed; the trusted validator's 27 files still match
their pin. The artifact audit checks complete grids, saved support identity,
source/binary hashes, matrices and relabelings. **5,360 witness documents** were
saved across the 75 timed configurations and 12 finite diagnostic configurations.
Neither timed grid returned a late event. Individual audits accompany each run.

All work remains local and uncommitted. No verifier, leaderboard entry, CI or
publication workflow was changed. These are witness searches, not full candidate
gate passes or exact-distance certificates.
