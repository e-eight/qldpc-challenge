# Three candidate search approaches

**Complete:** 198 frozen main configurations and 18 separately labeled routing
diagnostics passed their audits, checking 21,263 saved witness documents. The
candidate and routing suites passed 33 synthetic unit tests. Measured sources and
binaries remained identical across the three main grids.

**Recommendation:** prioritize a cheap, bounded structure detector that routes
to the existing full single-block search, with a generic native fallback. This
restores useful searches under hidden column layouts. Redesign the reduced
algebraic spaces before expanding their compute budget; profile and amortize the
decoder's setup before judging that broader family of methods. None of the new
candidates improves the frozen best reference bound on the three original hard
inputs. The new practical gain is recovering access to known structural shortcuts.

Three isolated implementations were developed and reviewed in parallel:

- [Matrix structure recovery](../../strategy_prototypes/matrix_structure_v2/README.md):
  infer column translations from check-row incidence, complete missing check
  orbits, and transfer logical representatives under verified sector exchanges.
- [Reduced algebraic spaces](../../strategy_prototypes/reduced_space_v2/README.md):
  packed C++ search over coupled polynomial quotient generators and separately
  labeled single-block spaces, combining up to four basis rows with pruning.
- [Decoder search](../../strategy_prototypes/decoder_search_v2/README.md):
  BP+OSD solves the zero stabilizer syndrome with an additional nonzero logical
  parity constraint, with randomized detectors, coordinates and channel costs.

Timed configurations run serially on CPU0 with one search thread. All methods
receive identical common logical initialization inside the deadline. Candidate
methods that finish early or are inapplicable give the remaining sector time to
a fresh guided search. Reports distinguish these fallback results from actual
candidate outputs. The controls are native incremental, guided, and the existing
single-block circulant search. Reference supports and target weights are used
only for evaluation and validation, never as search inputs.

The [frozen plan](../../strategy_prototypes/CANDIDATES_PLAN.md) specifies
198 configurations: seven original inputs × six methods × three seeds at
10 seconds/code, six relabeled hard inputs × six methods × one seed at
10 seconds/code, and three original hard inputs × six methods × two fresh seeds
at 60 seconds/code. Each budget splits equally between X and Z. Long runs are
independent, not extensions of short runs. Native calls can overrun; every late
valid export is retained but receives no deadline credit.

## Budget scaling on the three hard originals

Each cell is the median delivered upper bound at **10 seconds → 60 seconds**.
The short grid uses three seeds and the long grid uses two different seeds;
these are screening results, not a paired estimate of budget scaling.

| Code | Reference | Incremental | Guided | Circulant | Structure | Reduced | Decoder |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Board700 | 28 | 72 → 71.5 | 75 → 71 | 75 → 71 | 28 → 28 | 28 → 28 | 89 → 89 |
| Board682 | 72 | 84 → 77.5 | 82 → 80.5 | 76 → 76 | 82 → 80.5 | 88 → 77 | 96 → 96 |
| Regression690 | 28 | 83 → 80 | 82 → 80.5 | 48 → 48 | 82 → 80.5 | 56 → 56 | 93 → 93 |

Circulant on Board700 and structure on the two full bicycle cases get their
improvements through guided fallback. The reduced method's improvements come
from single-block branches. Its coupled branch improves initialization in **0 of
30 hard-case sectors** across both budgets. On Board682, incremental's two long
runs return 81 and 74, demonstrating substantial seed variation and retaining a
reason to keep multiple native search strategies available.

The [full tables](TABLES.md), [machine-readable aggregation](comparison.json),
and [60-second report](hard-60s/REPORT.md) retain all methods and per-seed results.
The [long decoder analysis](hard-60s-decoder-analysis.md) confirms that more time
permits timely decoder outputs, but none improves the common hard-case starting
bound. Board682 and Regression690 still complete only three trials per sector,
with the final trial arriving late. This is a negative result for these settings,
not a general rejection of decoder-based distance search.

## Short-budget findings across the corpus

The [seven-code screen](screen-10s/REPORT.md) contains 126 configurations and
10,791 saved witness documents. All 36 [relabeling controls](relabel-10s/REPORT.md)
also passed their audit, checking another 2,066 saved documents.

- Both structural candidates reach 28 on Board700, versus guided's median 75.
  Structure recovery delivers the first X witness in about 50–54 ms, including
  preparation and initialization. The reduced candidate's gain comes from its
  single-block branch, not its coupled branch.
- The existing circulant search remains strongest on Board682 and Regression690:
  medians 76 and 48, versus reduced-space 88 and 56 and guided 82 on both.
- Structure recovery retains 28 after a random qubit permutation of Board700.
  It fails after independent check-row shuffles as well. It uses useful row-order
  clues; this is not general code-equivalence recovery.
- Unproductive structure proposals can consume the budget: Mitten's median
  worsens from guided 24 to 30. Cheap rejection and a capped analysis budget are
  necessary before making this a default dispatch path.
- The decoder wins none of 21 paired original-code runs against guided. Its
  Mitten outputs 28/24/26 are competitive but less reliable than guided 24/24/24.
  On Board682 and Regression690 its first trial exceeds the five-second sector
  deadline, so none of its outputs receives short-budget credit.
- Toric 20 and bicycle960's 60 already come from common initialization. Verified
  sector transfer improves bicycle960's Z bound from 180 to 60; it does not improve
  the code-level minimum of 60.

These are witnessed upper bounds. Case labels are identifiers, not authoritative
reference values: the frozen target for Board682 is 72 and Tanner's is 32.

## Separate exploratory routing diagnostic

After freezing the main comparison, a small [routing prototype](../../strategy_prototypes/structure_route_v2/README.md)
uses inferred two-cycle partitions to reorder columns for the **existing** native
single-block kernel. It bypasses only the old layout recognizer and checks the
actual kernel, logical parity and inverse mapping. It does not assume a proposed
permutation is a proven automorphism.

This is a separate 18-configuration, one-seed diagnostic at **2 seconds/code**,
including common preparation, recovery, native setup and delivery. It was chosen
after seeing the main screen and is excluded from the frozen ranking.

| Input | Existing circulant with fallback | Inferred-block routing with fallback |
|---|---:|---:|
| Board700 original | 75 | 28 |
| Board700 columns shuffled | 77 | 28 |
| Board700 columns and rows shuffled | 77 | 77 |
| Board682 original | 84 | 84 |
| Board682 columns shuffled | 84 | 85 |
| Board682 columns and rows shuffled | 84 | 84 |
| Regression690 original | 48 | 48 |
| Regression690 columns shuffled | 84 | 48 |
| Regression690 columns and rows shuffled | 84 | 84 |

The restored 28 and 48 are actual routed-kernel outputs, not initialization or
fallback. All row-shuffled cases use guided fallback. Board682 shows no quality
gain in this short diagnostic. The result supports structure-based routing for
hidden columns, while leaving row-order-independent recovery unresolved. See the
[complete routing report](route-2s/REPORT.md) for stage attribution and timing;
all 1,132 saved documents passed its separate audit.

## Why the candidates differ

The [exact reduced-space membership analysis](reduced-analysis/README.md) checks
12 existing witnesses against the actual matrices and reconstructed search spaces.
The coupled branch uses 94–97% of branch time in the short hard-case runs without
improving common initialization. All six analyzed short-screen weight 48 circulant witnesses
on Regression690 lie outside every proposed reduced space. This excludes those
particular witnesses; it does not prove that no equally light logical exists in
the reduced spaces. The full individual-block kernels are larger than the
common-factor construction searches. Space coverage and time allocation both
need attention before micro-optimizing this prototype.

The separate [post-hoc reference membership check](reference-membership/README.md)
finds that both stored Regression690 weight-28 references and Board682's Z
weight-72 reference are outside all three reduced spaces. Board700's stored
weight-28 references are inside the coupled space, but its coupled search fails
to find them; its successful single-block witnesses are different vectors.
Thus coverage and search quality are distinct problems. This check reads frozen
evaluation witnesses only after algorithm freeze and supplies nothing to search.
It does not prove the absence of all equally light representatives in any space.

The [decoder analysis](screen-10s-decoder-analysis.md) separates construction,
decoding and deadline overruns. One trial takes roughly 12–14 seconds on Board682
and Regression690, approximately half in construction and half in decoding.
Every observed BP run uses all 100 iterations without convergence; OSD supplies
the valid output. The added logical checks are much denser than original checks,
but attributing the failure or setup cost specifically to density remains an
inference. Reusing prepared decoders and trying lighter detectors require a new
controlled experiment; they are not measured improvements here.

## Next experiments worth running

1. Give structure analysis a small explicit time allocation and a cheap rejection
   rule, route useful partitions to full individual-block kernels, and preserve
   generic native search for unsupported inputs. Extend recovery beyond useful
   check-row ordering. Repeat routing with more seeds and held-out codes before
   selecting a default dispatcher.
2. For a more ambitious algebraic follow-up, include the missing parts of the
   kernel decomposition and allocate time by wall clock rather than branch
   visits. Use the post-hoc membership findings to distinguish coverage from
   search difficulty, while keeping reference witnesses out of the next search.
   The current coupled beam should not receive a large runtime expansion.
3. If revisiting decoding, profile native construction versus BP/OSD, amortize a
   prepared decoder across several reliability perturbations, and test lighter
   logical detectors. Verify that parameter updates actually reach both BP and
   OSD. These changes deserve an independent comparison, particularly on Mitten.

## Evidence and reproduction

Each grid retains original and relabeled matrices, actual permutations, source
archives, source/binary SHA256 pins, raw timestamped X/Z support logs, per-run
records, and independent validation/persistence counts. Every distinct exported
support is saved through the existing research kit; late and non-improving
outputs are preserved too. The only packaging exemption is the existing size
cap exemption for fixtures with more than 700 qubits. No trusted verifier or
leaderboard file changed, no full submission gate ran, and no exact distance is
claimed.

Package versions are in [environment-freeze.txt](environment-freeze.txt), including
ldpc 2.4.1, SciPy 1.16.1 and NumPy 2.2.6. [hardware.json](hardware.json) records the
VM CPU/cache report. Native workspace counters exclude Python/NumPy allocations
and are not process RSS. The main source snapshots contain 106 files per grid.

The main grids allocate 3,780 seconds of search budgets. Actual timed search
elapsed totals 4,027.88 seconds (4,021.85 CPU seconds), mostly because coarse
decoder calls overrun. All 83 late main-grid exports and 16 late routing exports
are retained without deadline credit. These totals exclude independent witness
validation/persistence, imports, matrix loading and synthetic warmup.

The common runner and audit entry points are:

```sh
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/study_candidates.py --output <new-output>
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/report_candidates.py <completed-output>
.venv-benchmark/bin/python benchmarks/distance/audit_candidates.py <completed-output>
```

Run each prototype's documented build first. The grid runner archives its exact
settings; the frozen plan lists the additional relabel and 60-second arguments.
Audits require the live measured sources/binaries and locally staged witness
documents. Raw supports and measured source archives are also retained in each
grid for later independent reconstruction.

The candidate wrapper and three prototype suites passed 27 synthetic unit tests;
the separate routing prototype passed six. The trusted validator integrity check
passed for all 27 pinned files. These checks complement the corpus witness audits;
they are not distance certificates.

For the combined synthetic suites, use importlib mode because several isolated
prototypes intentionally have the same test filename:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=benchmarks/distance .venv-benchmark/bin/python -m pytest --import-mode=importlib -q \
  benchmarks/distance/test_candidates.py \
  benchmarks/distance/strategy_prototypes/matrix_structure_v2 \
  benchmarks/distance/strategy_prototypes/reduced_space_v2 \
  benchmarks/distance/strategy_prototypes/decoder_search_v2 \
  benchmarks/distance/strategy_prototypes/structure_route_v2
```

[validation-summary.json](validation-summary.json) indexes completed checks and
[SHA256SUMS.json](SHA256SUMS.json) pins the final artifact files. The measured
source archives remain unchanged; analysis sources and their input hashes are
retained separately.
